Shortcuts & Instructions

This page is being read aloud automatically. You do not need to look at the screen - just listen. Tap anywhere to pause or resume listening controls at the bottom.

Talking to Netra

  • Say "Hey Netra" anytime, followed by what you want - Netra is always listening for this.
  • Double-tap anywhere on the screen to start giving a voice command immediately, without saying the wake word first.

Touch Gestures (work anywhere on screen)

  • Long-press and hold for about one second to turn object detection on or off.
  • Double-tap to start a voice command.
  • Triple-tap quickly to instantly read aloud any text in front of the camera - great for medicine labels and documents.

Emergency

  • Shake your phone firmly to immediately alert your emergency contacts with your location.
  • Say "Hey Netra, emergency" or "I need help now" to do the same thing by voice.

Useful things to say

  • "Take me to the library" - start walking directions to any place.
  • "Where is my phone" - hear where an object was last seen.
  • "Remember this place as bathroom" - save your current location with a name.
  • "Where is the bathroom" - get directions back to a saved place.
  • "Read this" or "what does this say" - read text aloud.
  • "What did I say" or "read my history" - hear your recent activity.
  • Ask any general question, like "what's the capital of France".

Opening the app without touching your phone screen

  • Voice assistant: say "Hey Google, open Netra" on Android, or "Hey Siri, open Netra" on iPhone - this works once the app has been installed to your home screen.
  • Back Tap: a phone setting that opens the app when you tap the back of your phone twice or three times. On Android, this is under Settings, System, Gestures, Quick Tap. On iPhone, it's under Settings, Accessibility, Touch, Back Tap. A sighted helper needs to set this up once.
  • NFC tag: a small sticker, on a keychain or cane for example, that opens the app the moment your phone touches it. A sighted helper can set this up once using a free NFC writing app.

Other buttons on the main screen

  • Home, Memory, History, and Settings are at the bottom of the screen for a sighted helper to use if needed.
  • Everything on this page can also be done by voice - you never need to find a specific button.
